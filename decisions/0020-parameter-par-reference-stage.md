## Source facts that determine the reference design

Central inspected the pinned code and found the following obligations:

- `allocateMemory` assigns array sizes, labels, link counts, `fitFlag`, `paramSet`, `err` and `val`; it does **not** initialize every `prefit` or `prefitErr`. `initialiseOne` also leaves `ne_sw_ifuncN` unwritten. A2's correction is not canonical initialization. The `fullSetup` and `realloc` arguments are present but unused in these implementations.
- `readValue` reads the rest of a line with `fgets` and scans at most three tokens. The one-token and fit-flag-only forms can retain a previous `prefitErr`; coordinate conversion then scales that field. Repeated ordinary assignments are sequential. A duplicate with a prior uncertainty therefore has a defined state-dependent meaning that differs from an unwritten first uncertainty.
- Binary128 `parse_longdouble` uses `strtoflt128` with no end-pointer validation. `readValue` first replaces D/d with e in selected tokens. Coordinate helpers instead parse and calculate in binary64, and have different delimiter, sign and partial-conversion behavior. Preserve these boundaries and expression order.
- Symbolic links are directional and order-sensitive. `DSHK PX` writes a stored value and obtains a different linked prefit value; the other link branches have their own write sets. `getParameterValue` has specific one-link formulas and terminates for more than one link. It does not recursively evaluate a general dependency graph.
- `checkAllSet` copies parameter metadata when defaulting epochs, handles ecliptic pairs and fit flags, remaps legacy clock strings, and then performs binary consistency checks. DDGR with any FB parameter terminates; ELL1 with FB1 and PBDOT disables PBDOT's set/fit flags. These source decisions remain unchanged.
- `readParfile`, `readSimpleParfile` and `readParfileGlobal` have different initialization, default and consistency paths. The global reader repeatedly reads `tpar[0]`; its temporary ecliptic parameters are not allocated. The full reader can fall back to a timing-file header and keeps `noread` outside the pulsar loop. These wrappers cannot be treated as interchangeable aliases.

These are source-inspected facts, not claims that all affected paths already have executable evidence. K supplies that evidence and records additional findings without repairing the source.
