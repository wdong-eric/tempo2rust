## Required behavior families

1. **Clock directives and offsets.** Pass the actual `CLK_CORR_CHAIN` remainder to the real clock implementation. Exercise valid chains, repeated definitions, empty/missing/disconnected chains and cross-pulsar process history with declared local resources. Capture an observable route result where needed; J remains the authority for its already accepted graph corpus. For `AVERAGERES`, retain float precision and the order that enables the control before scans. For `TOFFSET`, preserve six-field scans, prior values on conversion failure, LF-only trimming, count increments and flags taken from the first `-` anywhere in the remainder, including a negative numeric value. Respect the actual fixed capacities.

2. **JUMP, SATJUMP and FD variants.** Preserve one-based preincremented counts, the complete stored remainder, fit defaults, conditional value scans and retained flags/errors. Ordinary JUMP does not clear a prior SAT flag; SATJUMP sets it. Exercise MJD/FREQ, NAME/TEL, flag and SCALED selectors and unknown selectors where defined. The apparent optional fit token is read from an uninitialized local when MJD/FREQ has exactly four scanned tokens, or NAME/TEL/flag/SCALED has exactly three; those paths are unrun. Shorter branches that do not reach that read can retain initialized destination values. FD forms preserve the ordered FD-number test, FDJUMP_SCALE/FDJUMPLOG comparisons, FDJUMPDM index -2, prefix parsing, PINT-format side effects, index-zero nonconsumption and the absence of a dedicated SCALED parse branch. Do not inherit the ordinary FD guard as proof that its destination index is valid.

3. **Waves and sampled corrections.** Cover WAVE_SCALE, WAVE_SIN/COS/combined records, WAVDM, QUAD, ORBIFUNC, IFUNC, telescope clock/position/velocity samples and both QIFUNC families/positions. Exercise first/last valid indices, sparse and repeated records, retained intermediate cells, maxima/count updates, value/error scans, IFUNC weights and velocity flags. Dispatch is a mixture of case-insensitive equality and case-sensitive substring tests: lowercase wave names can enter the first sine branch, and lowercase qifunc names can enter the earlier IFUNC branch. Only execute spellings with a defined parsed index. Preserve exact STEL exclusions, actual pre-write ORBIFUNC/IFUNC upper checks, asymmetric telescope lower checks and their diagnostic text. Missing bounds are not permission for an out-of-bounds golden.

4. **DM/CM/solar-wind records.** Test DMMODEL's count resets and ordered DMOFF/_DM/_CM/_NE_SW histories, including independently seeded counters. DMOFF appends both families, discards its parsed error, sets CM value/error to zero and both weights to one. _DM also discards error; _CM and _NE_SW initialize a local error to zero and retain supplied errors. Preserve scan behavior and all untouched tails. The count tests after writes do not provide safe overflow experiments; the last valid write is distinct from the subsequent invalid append. Natural uninitialized solar-wind count use remains unrun.

5. **Constraints.** Inventory every CONSTRAIN name and its ordered sequence, aliases, duplicates, unknown names, AUTO and CONSTRAINT_EFACTOR. These are independent if statements: DMMODEL_DM1 accumulates its individual term plus the common and annual groups. Special constraints retain the raw remainder and allocated bytes. IFUNC_COV overwrites the copied terminator with a newline and leaves the extra allocated byte unwritten. Observe only its known initialized prefix and allocation/write extent; do not call strlen or serialize it as a completed C string, and do not add a terminator. Missing fgets results and capacity overflow are unrun. Preserve ownership/destruction behavior without treating allocator addresses as scientific output.

6. **GW compound records.** Cover the single, circular/eccentric, memory, cosmic-string and burst field groups, source binary64 scans, repeated/partial conversions and interaction with their ordinary parameter records. GWECC_PSRTERM changes parameter labels only when paramSet is exactly one; include both record orders and other signed flags. Parsing these controls does not establish correctness or Rust transfer of their physical models.

7. **Noise controls and events.** Cover T2/TN flag lists, global controls, aliases, all red/DM/SW/chromatic/quasiperiodic scalar controls, band/group noise and DM/shapelet events. Preserve stored units and raw counts rather than applying preprocessing here. Test subtraction packing with base-zero strtoull parsing, masking, prior orders, clamping and order/flag updates in different sequences. 8. **Shapelet and stream boundaries.** Preserve negative/zero counts, the MAX_TNShapeCoef clamp, actual number of coefficients consumed, optional fit-flag retention and the frequency-scale check before the event count increment. A failed coefficient scan prints a zeroing warning but does not zero the remaining coefficients. Use explicit initialized tails to demonstrate this. Include extra coefficients becoming later fit/dispatch tokens, repeat events and defined partial prefixes on invalid-scale exits. Missing uninitialized locals and out-of-capacity event writes remain unrun.

9. **Real reader integration.** Exercise representative mixed specialized files through all three actual readers, repeated calls and two pulsars. Retain the full reader's reset of nPhaseJump/nJumps and links, its non-reset of nfdJumps and other append counters, and the simple/global readers' different reset/finalization behavior.
